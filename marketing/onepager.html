AI & UK Property

The Daily Briefing — a listening app for the daily podcast

A free app for following Dezrez's daily podcast on how AI is changing UK estate agency and conveyancing. Each episode is a short two-host discussion drawn from that day's industry news. The app puts the whole series in one place — searchable, sortable, and ready to pick up wherever you left off.

What it does

The whole archive, always current

Opens on every episode published to date, newest first, refreshed each time you open it.

Search as you type

Filter the back catalogue instantly by title, description or date, with matches highlighted.

Order it your way

Sort by episode or by date, oldest or newest first, whichever suits how you catch up.

Picks up where you stopped

Remembers your position in every episode and marks off the ones you have finished.

Listen at your own pace

Adjust playback speed, skip back and forward, or download an episode to keep.

Fits around a working day

Keeps working without a connection, installs to the desktop, and answers the usual media keys.

Who it is for. Estate agents, conveyancers and the teams around them — anyone working in UK residential property who wants to keep up with how AI is changing their work, in the gaps of a busy day. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to pay.
