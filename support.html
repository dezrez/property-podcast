Before you write in

Most problems clear up with a refresh. Press R, or the refresh button at the top right, to re-read the feed. If that does not help, the answers below cover the things that come up most.

Common questions

Why do some episodes say "superseded" or "2 versions"?

Because the podcast feed genuinely contains them twice. Four episodes — 20 to 23 July 2026 — were re-recorded and re-published under the same episode ID rather than a new one, so the feed lists 33 entries for 29 actual episodes.

By default the app shows the most recently published version of each and tells you how many it merged. Turn off Merge duplicate GUIDs in the toolbar to see every entry, with the older ones labelled superseded. Nothing is hidden from you — it is just tidied by default.

An episode will not play

One older duplicate entry in the feed points at a broken address and will not load. If you have turned duplicate merging off and picked an entry labelled superseded, try the current version of the same episode instead.

Otherwise, check that you are online and that your device is not blocking richiep540.github.io, which is where the audio is hosted.

Why is the artwork the app icon rather than the show's cover?

The feed advertises a cover image that is not currently published — the address it gives returns "not found". Rather than show a broken image, the app falls back to its own icon. If the publisher adds the cover art, the app will pick it up automatically with no update needed.

Why do some episode lengths have a "~" in front?

The feed does not include episode durations. Once you play an episode, the app measures its true length and remembers it. For episodes you have not played, it estimates the length from the file size and marks it ~. Exact figures have no tilde.

Does it work offline?

Yes, for browsing. The app and the last-loaded episode list are cached, so it opens, sorts and searches with no connection, showing "Offline · cached" at the top. Audio still needs a connection, since episodes are streamed rather than stored. Use the download button on an episode to keep a copy of the MP3.

How do I install it as a desktop app?

Open the app in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome and use the install icon in the address bar, or the browser menu: Edge — ⋯ → Apps → Install this site as an app; Chrome — ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app.

You get its own window, a Start-menu entry and a taskbar icon, it works offline, and hardware media keys control playback. Uninstall it like any other app.

How do I clear my listening history?

Everything the app remembers is stored on your own device. Clear site data for this site in your browser settings, or uninstall the installed app, and it is all gone. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is stored.

Reporting a problem

So we can help quickly, please include as much of this as you can:

  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • The episode involved, if it is specific to one — the date or the ID shown under the title, such as episode-2026-08-20.
  • Your browser and version, and whether you are using it in a browser tab or as an installed app.
  • Whether a refresh made any difference.
About the podcast itself

We maintain the app, not the show. Questions about episode content, scheduling or the feed's own contents are best directed to the podcast's publisher. If you are not sure which applies, write to us anyway and we will point you in the right direction.
