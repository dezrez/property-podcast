Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

This app collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is no server of ours to send it to.

Who this policy is from

The app is published by Dezrez. It is a listening client for the AI & UK Property: The Daily Briefing podcast. This policy covers the app itself — the pages served from dezrez.github.io/property-podcast.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no server, no database and no analytics for this app. It is a set of static files that run entirely in your browser. We have no way of knowing who you are, that you used the app, or what you listened to.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves a small amount of data in your browser's local storage so it can remember your place between sessions. This never leaves your device and is never transmitted anywhere:

WhatWhy
Theme, sort order, playback speed, volume, duplicate-merging preferenceSo the app looks and behaves the way you left it
Playback position for each episode, and which are finishedSo you can resume where you stopped
A cached copy of the podcast feedSo the app opens instantly and works offline
Measured episode durationsSo lengths do not have to be re-read every time

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing site data for this site in your browser, or by uninstalling the app if you installed it. It is removed immediately and permanently. We hold no copy, because we never had one.

Network connections the app makes

The app contacts only these, all static files:

  • Its own files (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, icons) from dezrez.github.io, hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • The podcast feed at richiep540.github.io/ai-property-podcast/feed.xml, to list episodes.
  • Episode audio files from the same host, when you press play or download.

As with visiting any website, the servers hosting those files receive a standard web request that includes your IP address. Both are served by GitHub, under GitHub's privacy statement rather than ours. We receive nothing from those requests and have no access to any logs of them.

Cookies and tracking

The app sets no cookies. It uses no analytics, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting and no advertising networks. There is no third-party script of any kind in the app.

Sharing your information

We cannot share what we do not have. No data is sold, rented, shared or disclosed to anyone, because none is collected.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights to access, correct, and erase personal data held about you. As we hold no personal data about users of this app, there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase. The data described above sits on your own device and is entirely under your control. If you would like to discuss this, contact us using the details below.

Children

The app is a general-audience podcast player about UK property and technology. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with an updated date at the top.
