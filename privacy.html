Privacy Policy

AI & UK Property: The Daily Briefing — last updated 20 August 2026

The short version

This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is no server to send it to.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally, in your browser's storage, so it can remember your place between sessions. This never leaves your device:

  • Your display preferences — theme, sort order, playback speed, volume, and whether duplicate episodes are merged.
  • Your listening position for each episode, and which episodes you have finished.
  • A cached copy of the podcast feed, so the app opens and works without a connection.
  • Measured episode durations, so lengths do not have to be re-read each time.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing site data for it, removes all of this permanently. We have no copy of it.

Network connections the app makes

The app contacts exactly two things, both hosted on GitHub Pages:

  • The podcast feed at richiep540.github.io/ai-property-podcast/feed.xml, to list episodes.
  • The episode audio files on the same host, when you press play or download.

As with any web request, the host serving those files (GitHub) will see your IP address and receive a standard request. That is governed by GitHub's privacy statement, not by us. We receive nothing from those requests.

Children

The app is a general-audience podcast player. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
